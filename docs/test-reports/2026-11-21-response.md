# Response to the 21 Nov real-phone test (detector fixes)

Thanks: the report and the full log made all three fixes straightforward. Fixed in the detector (`android/`):

## 1. Wrong person asked (call 1 asked Daughter)
- **Asking someone's phone now needs a clear match:** distance ≤ **0.92** (match limit 1.00 minus 0.08), **and** the best person
  must beat the next-closest person by ≥ **0.05**. Weaker matches (0.92–1.00) count as "not sure yet", so nobody is asked and
  no "This may NOT be…" warning is shown. Still **2 of 3 frames**.
- **The log now shows the top two**, e.g. `Face ≈ son (distance 0.79) (next: daughter 1.12)`, or
  `Face maybe daughter (distance 0.97), not sure (next: son 1.01)`.
- Replaying your log through the rules (distances from `2026-11-21-detector-log.txt`):

  | Rule | Call 1 (close-up from below) | Call 2 | Call 3 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | old: ≤ 1.00, 2 of 3 | ❌ Daughter after 2.6 s | Son 1.4 s | Son 2.4 s |
  | ≤ 0.90, 3 of 4 (your suggestion) | Son 35.3 s | Son 6.6 s | Son 3.5 s |
  | **new: ≤ 0.92, 2 of 3** | **Son 8.7 s** | **Son 1.4 s** | **Son 2.4 s** |

  The margin check isn't in the replay (the old log didn't print the second-best person), so real behaviour may be a little
  stricter. The limit moves with the *Match strictness* slider (the ask-limit is always slider − 0.08).

## 2. HANG UP needed 3 taps
If the end-call button isn't in the accessibility tree (WhatsApp hid its controls), the detector now hides its card, taps the
middle of the call screen (`dispatchGesture`, needs `canPerformGestures`, already in the config) to bring the controls back,
waits 450 ms and looks again. If it still can't find it, a red card says to tap the red button.
**After installing the new APK, turn the call watcher off and on once** so Android grants the gesture capability.

## 3. 15 s too short
Now a setting in section 4, **Time to answer on their phone**, 10–60 s, **default 30 s**.
