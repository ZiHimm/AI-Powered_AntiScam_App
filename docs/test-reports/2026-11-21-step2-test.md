## W4: faces are never sent (blocking)

**Symptom:** after the wizard's face step, the "My family" cards still show "no face yet" for Son and Dad.

**Evidence from Dad's phone** ([log](2026-11-21-step2-dad-logcat.txt)): the face is added three times, twice while
connected to the family, and `Shared my face (…) with …` never appears; nor does `✓ <name> shared their face` for anyone else.
```
08:08:33.672  Dad's phone connected. Device key 32:40:f3:ac:19:ab
08:08:33.685  Son's phone is online
08:13:51.381  Daughter's phone is online
08:24:07.081  Added 3 face samples for Dad from the camera      ← wizard face step (owner); no "Shared my face"
09:23:25.011  Added 3 face samples for dad from the camera      ← retake from settings; relay was down here (doesn't count)
09:29:28.859  Dad's phone connected. Device key 32:40:f3:ac:19:ab
09:29:29.681  Son's phone is online
09:29:30.711  Daughter's phone is online
09:31:12.706  Added 3 face samples for dad from the camera      ← retake while connected; no "Shared my face"
```

**Evidence from the relay** ([trace](2026-11-21-step2-relay-trace.txt), from the 09:29 reconnect): profiles **are** sent and
delivered both ways, but **not a single `face` message** in the whole trace, including after Dad's retake at 09:31:12.
```
[9:29:29] son -> dad: profile          [9:29:30] daughter -> son: profile
[9:29:29] dad -> son: profile          [9:29:30] daughter -> dad: profile
                                        [9:29:30] dad -> daughter: profile
                                        [9:29:30] son -> daughter: profile
(+ config messages; 0 × "face", before or after 09:31:12)
```

**What this rules out / points to** (from reading `FaceShare.kt`, `FamilyLink.kt`, `FaceStore.kt` on the branch):
- Not the relay: it delivers profiles both ways, and its `check.js` passes.
- Not signatures: a bad profile signature would log `✗ Ignored a profile … bad signature`, and none appears.
- Not the owner flag on disk: `FaceStore` saves and loads `owner`, and both enrol paths pass `EXTRA_OWNER=true`
  (wizard `SetupActivity:241`).
- **Likely `FaceShare.sendMyFace()` returning silently** at one of its three guards: `!FamilyLink.connected`, no owner face
  found, or `targets.isEmpty()` (no member with `online && v2`). In the trace each phone sends exactly **one** profile to each
  other phone and **no reply profile**, so on receipt `onProfile` saw `first == false` (already `v2`?). If `v2` were true and
  the members online, the 09:31 retake should have produced a `face` message. Worth logging which guard returns.
- Possibly related: the retakes from settings were saved under **"dad"** (lowercase) while the wizard saved **"Dad"**
  (`addSample` matches case-insensitively, so same person; noting it in case the owner lookup or display differs).

**Suggestion:** log once per call of `sendMyFace` why nothing was sent (not connected / no owner face / no step-2 member
online), and log `onProfile` (first or repeat, member now `v2`). Then a single retake shows the cause.

## UX notes (wizard and home)
- **"Your name in the family"** offers quick-picks **Mum / Dad / Son / Daughter**; the tester tapped **Daughter** instead of
  typing "Aisha". The quick-picks steer people to the old fixed roles, so custom names never got tested. Maybe show the
  quick-picks as examples only, or put the text field first with a clearer label.
- **"Check my incoming video calls — Recommended for seniors. Others can leave it off."** is clear. A senior may not
  understand "guardians" in "My guardians are alerted if a scammer targets me" (suggestion: "My family is alerted…").
- The face cards say **"no face yet"** even right after the face step, with no hint why or what to do (the W4 bug), which is
  confusing. Once fixed, a short "sent to N family members" confirmation after the selfies would help.
